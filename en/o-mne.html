MY EXPERIENCE · SINCE 2012

Care with a personal touch

My name is Mária Hulmanová, and I have been working professionally as a massage therapist since 2012. I combine years of hands-on experience with an attentive, individual approach to every client.

Between 2021 and 2025, I spent four years working in the rehabilitation department of a polyclinic in Ružinov, Bratislava. This experience has shaped the way I approach massage and musculoskeletal care.

I specialise mainly in therapeutic massage, combining different methods according to each client's needs, particularly soft tissue techniques and myofascial massage techniques. I tailor every session to your individual needs and comfort.

I look forward to welcoming you to Masáže Majka at the Student House Hotel complex, Tomášikova 34, Bratislava.
