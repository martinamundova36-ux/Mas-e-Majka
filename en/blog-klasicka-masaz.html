MASSAGE GUIDE

Classical massage: when is it right for you?

Classical massage is a hands-on treatment used for relaxation and relief of muscle tension.

What can it help with?

Massage can support relaxation and provide short-term relief of some types of pain and discomfort. Responses vary from person to person, and it should not replace appropriate medical care.

What happens during a session?

Tell your massage therapist about areas of discomfort, your preferred pressure and any medical conditions. The treatment can be adjusted to your comfort.

When should you seek advice first?

Consult a healthcare professional before massage if you have an acute injury, fever, a suspected blood clot, a skin infection or another condition that may make massage unsuitable.

Further reading: NIH NCCIH – Massage therapy.
