MASSAGE GUIDE

Soft tissue techniques: gentle manual care

Soft tissue techniques are hands-on approaches that focus on the skin, fascia and other soft tissues.

What does research show?

A 2024 review of systematic reviews in JAMA Network Open found that most conclusions about massage for painful conditions had low or very low certainty. Some findings had moderate certainty; benefits cannot be guaranteed. Results for massage in general cannot establish the effectiveness of every soft tissue technique.

Source: Mak et al. (2024), Use of Massage Therapy for Pain, 2018–2023.

What can you expect?

The practitioner uses gentle movements and pressure suited to your needs and comfort. The aim is to support relaxation and ease feelings of tension.

Who might enjoy them?

People who prefer a gentler approach may find these techniques comfortable. Their suitability depends on individual circumstances and health conditions.

Important information

Seek medical advice first for an acute injury, fever, inflammation, unexplained or worsening pain, or a serious medical condition. Evidence varies across different manual techniques. They are not a replacement for medical evaluation or evidence-based treatment when needed.

Further reading: NIH NCCIH – Massage therapy.
