2. Personal data and purposes

When requesting an appointment you may provide your name, phone number, email, selected service, preferred date and time, and an optional note. We use these details to respond to your request. The website does not send booking form data to our server. It opens your email application with a draft that you decide whether to send. Sent messages are processed by the sender's and recipient's email providers. Please do not include health details or other sensitive information in your note.

3. Recipients and retention

Email and hosting providers may process data as necessary to provide their services. The following planned retention periods must also be followed in the controller's actual email and record-management practices:

  • Unsuccessful enquiries and unconfirmed appointments: no longer than 3 months after the last communication, unless needed for a specific dispute.
  • Correspondence relating to completed appointments and services: no longer than 12 months after the service, unless a longer statutory period or the protection of legal claims applies.
  • Accounting records: 10 years following the year to which they relate, where the controller is subject to Section 35 of Slovak Act No. 431/2002 Coll.; any applicable longer statutory period prevails.
  • Cookie preferences: stored in your browser until you delete them; changing your choice overwrites the previous record.

After the applicable period, data will be deleted or anonymised unless the law or an ongoing legal dispute requires further retention. Publishing these periods does not automatically delete emails; the controller must apply them in practice.

4. Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing, depending on the legal basis. Contact us above or lodge a complaint with the Slovak Data Protection Authority (dataprotection.gov.sk).
